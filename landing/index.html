How it works

A missed call becomes a booked job — on its own.

The whole recovery loop runs while you're on the tools. You only step in when there's something worth your time.

  1. 1

    The call comes in — and you can't answer

    You're up a ladder, under a sink, or already with a customer.

  2. 2

    Zippy texts back instantly

    Seconds after the missed call, they get a reply in your voice — before they scroll down and call the next name on the list.

  3. 3

    The lead lands in your inbox

    Caller, time, and text-back status all in one place, alongside your SMS, web chat, Messenger and Instagram conversations.

  4. 4

    Zippy follows up and offers times

    It answers the usual questions, proposes slots from your calendar, and keeps the conversation warm until they commit.

  5. 5

    You get the booking

    The appointment appears on your calendar and you're notified — with the full conversation already attached to the contact.
